Arthroscopy & Sports Injury Care

Arthroscopy is a surgical technique that uses a camera and small instruments through small incisions to assess or treat selected joint problems. Whether it is appropriate depends on the diagnosis and evidence for the proposed procedure.

Sports injuries need an accurate assessment

Pain, swelling, locking or instability can arise from different structures. History and examination guide whether imaging or specialist assessment is needed.

What is arthroscopy used for?

Arthroscopy may be used for selected meniscal, ligament and other joint conditions. Not every finding on an MRI requires surgery, and some problems are best managed without an operation.

Rehabilitation matters

Recovery depends on the procedure, any repair performed and the person's health. Follow the specific protocol provided by the surgeon and physiotherapist.

Questions before surgery

Ask what problem the procedure is intended to address, what non-operative options exist, likely benefits and risks, recovery expectations and what happens if you wait.

2. Exercise and movement library

These are examples of common exercise categories, not a personalised rehabilitation protocol. Choose an area to filter the educational list.

Gentle knee bending and straightening

Purpose: practise comfortable range of movement.

General approach: move slowly within a comfortable range; stop if pain significantly increases.

Avoid without guidance after a recent injury or operation if your clinician has restricted movement.

Quadriceps muscle setting

Purpose: learn to gently activate the front thigh muscle.

General approach: with the leg supported, gently tighten the thigh without forcing the knee; relax between efforts.

Not a substitute for a prescribed post-operative programme.

Supported standing hip movement

Purpose: practise controlled hip movement while holding a stable support.

General approach: keep the trunk upright and move only through a comfortable range.

Follow hip precautions if you have had hip surgery.

Ankle pumps

Purpose: gently move the ankle up and down when permitted.

General approach: move slowly without forcing the joint.

After surgery or injury, follow your clinician's weight-bearing and movement instructions.

ACL rehabilitation: phase-based care

Purpose: understand that ACL rehabilitation progresses through clinician-guided phases.

General approach: progress is based on swelling, range of motion, strength, control and functional criteria—not a calendar alone.

Do not start running, pivoting or return-to-sport drills without clearance from your treating team.

Gentle shoulder pendulum education

Purpose: learn about a low-effort movement sometimes used when appropriate.

General approach: only use if advised and move gently without forcing the shoulder.

Not appropriate for every shoulder injury or after every operation.

Stop if you develop sharp or worsening pain, new swelling, dizziness or unusual symptoms. Recent injuries and post-operative rehabilitation require individual instructions.

Common questions

Before your appointment

What should I bring to my consultation?

Bring previous prescriptions, scan or X-ray reports, relevant medical records, a list of medicines and any questions you would like to discuss.

Can I discuss robotic knee or hip replacement?

Yes. You can discuss whether joint replacement or a robotic-assisted approach may be appropriate. Suitability depends on your diagnosis, examination and individual circumstances.

Can I book an appointment online?

Use the Book Appointment links on this website to open Dr. Shahid's EKA Care profile and check the available booking options.

What if I have a serious injury?

For a major injury, severe bleeding, deformity, loss of sensation or a limb that becomes cold or pale, seek urgent medical attention at the nearest emergency department.
